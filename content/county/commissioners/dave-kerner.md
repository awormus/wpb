## Background
- Elected to the Board of County Commissioners in November 2016; a Palm Beach County native raised in unincorporated District 3.
- Before the Commission, served two terms in the Florida House of Representatives (ranking member of the Judiciary Committee); earlier a police officer and special prosecutor in Palm Beach County.
- University of Florida graduate (B.A., 2006; J.D., 2010); civil trial attorney since 2010 (per the page).
