## Background
- Yale graduate (B.A., American Studies) and former U.S. Navy fighter pilot who flew 85 combat missions in Vietnam; later earned an MBA from Wharton and worked about ten years as an investment banker in New York.
- Moved to Palm Beach Gardens in 1993 and ran several businesses; served on the Northern Palm Beach County Improvement District board and two terms on the Palm Beach Gardens City Council.
- Appointed by the Governor to the Enterprise Florida board in November 2009.
- Elected to the District 1 seat in 2012 and re-elected in 2016.
