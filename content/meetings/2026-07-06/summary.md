# Summary — 2026-07-06 regular meeting

Outcomes below are from the official **pass/fail** sheet (not minutes). Agenda text is in `agenda.md`.

## Outcomes (pass/fail)

- **Appointments:** all approved (incl. DDA appointment of Craig Glover).
- **Consent:** all items approved (streetscape/FDOT maintenance; water-main appropriation Res. 130-26(F); EMS grant; settlement; utility liens; grant ratification).
- **[Ord. 5172-26](./agenda.md#item-9-1) (2nd reading):** approved — amend/restate public art program; create Chapter 43 (Public Art); relocate Chapter 78 / mural provisions.
- **[Ord. 5173-26](./agenda.md#item-9-2) (2nd reading):** approved — BMUD townhome clarifications; Currie MUD height-bonus / Currie Park incentive changes.
- **[Ords. 5174-26 / 5175-26 / 5176-26](./agenda.md#item-9-3) (1st reading):** approved — voluntary annexation + FLUM Multifamily + MF32 zoning for ~8.54 acres at **5710 North Haverhill Road** (2nd reading Jul 20).
- **[Ord. 5177-26](./agenda.md#item-9-4) (1st reading):** approved — six-month Zoning in Progress for specified MF32 Flagler/Washington corridor parcels (2nd reading Jul 20).
