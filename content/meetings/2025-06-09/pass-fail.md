THORNTON NURSERIES, INC.; WAIVING THE PROCUREMENT REQUIREMENTS PURSUANT TO SECTION 66-94 OF THE CITY CODE OF ORDINANCES; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

#### Background Information

On April 14, 2025, Resolution No. 106-25(F) was approved by the City Commission to amend the general fund budget for the relocation of trees in Dreher Park. The City wishes to relocate ten (10) large specimen trees, planted by Paul Dreher.

Pursuant to a ground lease agreement approved by the City Commission, the Boys & Girls Club is developing a club facility within Dreher Park. The City would like to enter into an agreement with the contractor who will be on-site planting and moving large trees for the Boys and Girls Club.

The contractor has the tree removal scheduled in the next few weeks. To facilitate the survival of relocated trees, there is insufficient time for the City to go through the formal procurement solicitation process to obtain a contractor to transplant large canopy trees.

The City believes economic efficiencies can be achieved by utilizing the same contractor to remove and relocate the ten (10) trees within Dreher Park. J.D. Thornton Nurseries, Inc. provided a proposal to transplant the trees at a cost of $108,910.

The City wishes to engage J.D. Thornton Nurseries, Inc. to relocate the ten (10) trees within Dreher Park. Pursuant to Section 66-94 of the Procurement Code, the Commission must approve contracts in excess of $50,000 that have not been competitively selected.

Resolution No. 128-25 waives the requirement for the procurement solicitation process and approves the agreement with J.D. Thornton Nurseries, Inc. to relocate the trees.

#### Background Information

Pursuant to the Florida Contraband Forfeiture Act, when a law enforcement agency obtains title to the forfeited property, the agency shall use the funds to pay for court costs incurred in the forfeiture proceedings. After that, the remaining funds shall be used for school resource officers, crime prevention, safe neighborhood, drug abuse education and prevention programs, or other law enforcement purposes, which include defraying the cost of protracted or complex investigations, providing additional equipment or expertise, purchasing automated external defibrillators for use in law enforcement vehicles, and providing matching funds to obtain federal grants. These funds may be expended upon request by the Chief of Police to the City Commission and upon appropriation to the Police Department’s miscellaneous trust fund as the municipality’s governing body deems appropriate.
