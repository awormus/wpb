## 4. CIVILITY AND DECORUM

The City of West Palm Beach is committed to civility and decorum by its officials, employees and members of the public who attend this meeting. The City Code, Secs. 2-31(8), 2-31(18) and 2-31(22), provides in pertinent part:
- Officials shall be recognized by the Chair and shall not interrupt a speaker.
- Public comment shall be addressed to the City Commission as a whole and not to any individual on the dais or in the audience.
- Displays of anger, rudeness, ridicule, impatience, lack of respect and personal attacks are strictly prohibited.
- Unauthorized remarks from the audience, stamping of feet, whistles, yells and similar demonstrations shall not be permitted.
- Offenders may be removed from the meeting.
- Any person desiring to address the Commission shall file a written request with the city clerk prior to consideration of the matter by the Commission or prior to the public comment portion of a meeting. The person wishing to speak shall complete a comment card for each agenda item the person wishes to address, which shall include the person's full name, address, and the numbered agenda item. The person will not be recognized if the comment card is not completed.

The Marina Commercial Marine Planned Development (CMPD) is located along the east side of North Flagler Drive between 38th and 44th Streets and consists of ±14.202 acres of upland property and ±4.838 acres of submerged lands (totaling ±19.04 acres).

The property originally consisted of two (2) developments that had previously been reviewed and approved independently. The developments consisted of :
- Rybovich Marina Commercial Marine Planned Development (CMPD) ±13.986 acres located between 38th and 42nd Streets; and

- TRG Residential Planned Development (RPD) ±5.094 acres located between 42nd and 44th Streets

On April 28, 2014, the City Commission of the City of West Palm Beach approved the consolidation of these developments into one CMPD, known as The Marina, with the adoption of Resolution No. 20-14. The consolidation created a unified regulatory framework that balanced residential, commercial, and marine-related uses while creating a vibrant waterfront community.

A Level III Site Plan Review was previously approved for Permissible Building Areas (PBAs) #1, #2, and #3 on July 16, 2018, with the adoption of Resolution No. 186-18. This approval authorized the development of two (2) high-rise towers, both consisting of 24 stories and totaling 399 multi-family dwelling units and ±4,050 square feet of nonresidential uses.

On the ground floor of the garage, facing the waterfront, the development contains “active use”, serving as a liner to the parking garage and offering opportunities for retail and other non-residential amenities for residents, guests, and the surrounding neighborhood. The developed rental community was completed in 2023 and is now known as Icon at Marina Village. Consistent with the process outlined in the Design Guidelines, the applicant has submitted a Level III Site Plan Review for the second phase of development, encompassing PBAs #4, # 8, #9, and #10.

To respond to market and community needs, the proposal has provided complimentary uses such as marine amenities, retail, restaurants, flexible office space, and on-site amenities. The current project proposal responds to the current needs by providing the following: 1. PBA #4: Is proposed to be developed with a pool as part of the yacht crew amenities, along with a small building adjacent to the pool, which will provide restroom facilities.

2. PBA #8 & #9: Will consist of a unified parking structure which consists of 681 spaces with an above 147-unit multi-family residential tower, with guest suites and resident amenities. The residential tower is a total of 34 stories (422’) in height. The active uses were removed from the North Flagler frontage and are now internal to the site along the promenade. On the ground level of the garage, the following uses are proposed:
- A 60,937 square foot private/semi-private club with additional guest suites.

- 20,691 square feet of office space.

- Fronting onto the promenade are: i) crew amenities and gym (9,535 square feet); ii) a ship store/retail (3,740 square feet); iii) marina storage (1,855 square feet); and iv) general retail (7,805 square feet).

3. PBA #10: Will be developed with the applicant’s signature building within the Marina CMPD, providing 112 multi-family dwelling units with guest suites and resident amenities. The building is proposed at a total of 36 stories (461 feet) in height. Also, an 8,376-squarefoot restaurant fronting onto the piazza and promenade is proposed.

The applicant diverted from the previous approved master plan, which provided a frontage road and included provisions for active uses along N.

Flagler Drive. As previously mentioned, the active uses now occur on both sides of the promenade leading into the central piazza or central square.

The applicant has also proposed to reallocate the unused building height as an entitled and unused development height to the remaining unbuilt phases, which would increase the previously approved maximum height from 399 feet to 461 feet. To accommodate this request, the major amendment reflects an increase in the Design Guideline’s maximum height allowed to 450 feet.

Even though the applicant has proposed a net decrease in the cumulative building height across the Marina CMPD (for principal structures and parking garages), the unused height for the existing structures does not guarantee a right of reallocation for proposed building past the maximum approved height. Staff maintains that the height should retain the previously approved height for consistency with previously approved Planned Development and toaccount for the existing residential structures and districts east of the subject area.
