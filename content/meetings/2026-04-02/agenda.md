### 3.1. Swearing-in Ceremony for the re-elected and newly elected City

            Commissioners.
            Originating Department:
            Mayor's Office
            Background Information:
            The swearing-in ceremony for the re-elected and newly elected City
            Commissioners is being held pursuant to Sec. 2-1, Code of Ordinances,
            which reads: "Commencement of terms of office. The term of office of a
            newly elected mayor or newly elected city commissioner shall commence
            at 5:00 p.m. on the fourth Thursday immediately following the regular
            election."

            The Oath of Office will be administered to the re-elected and newly
            elected City Commissioners: Cathleen Ward (District 1), Christy Fox
            (District 3), and Stephen Sylvester (District 5).

            Fiscal Note:
            No                              fiscal                          impact.

<a id="item-3-2"></a>
### 3.2. Election of the City Commission President.

            Originating Department:
            Mayor's Office
            Background Information:
            The election of the City Commission President shall take place pursuant
            to Sec. 2.05 of the City's Charter, which reads: "Induction and meetings.
            After each annual city election, the newly elected members shall be
            inducted and assume office at a public meeting. At such meeting, the city
            commissioners shall elect one (1) of their number to the office of the
            president of the city commission, who shall serve at the pleasure of the
            city commission."

            Fiscal Note:
            No fiscal impact.

## 4. ADJOURNMENT

*Pursuant to Resolution No. 179-95, adopted according to the provisions of Section 286.0115, Florida
Statutes, members of the Commission shall disclose on the record: 1) ex-parte communications -
verbal or written; 2.) written communications shall be placed in the record; and 3) site visits,
investigations, etc. shall be disclosed.

NOTICE: If any person decides to appeal any decision made by the City Commission at this meeting,
that person will need a record of the proceedings, and that, for such purposes, may need to ensure
that a verbatim records of the proceedings be made, which record includes the testimony and
evidence upon which the appeal is based. The City of West Palm Beach does not prepare or provide
such record.
