## 2. CIVILITY AND DECORUM

       The City of West Palm Beach is committed to civility and decorum by its officials, employees
       and members of the public who attend this meeting. The City Code, Secs. 2-31(8), 2-31(18)
       and 2-31(22), provides in pertinent part:
          - Officials shall be recognized by the Chair and shall not interrupt a speaker.
          - Public comment shall be addressed to the City Commission as a whole and not to any
             individual on the dais or in the audience.
          - Displays of anger, rudeness, ridicule, impatience, lack of respect and personal attacks
             are strictly prohibited.
          - Unauthorized remarks from the audience, stamping of feet, whistles, yells and similar
             demonstrations shall not be permitted.
          - Offenders may be removed from the meeting.
          - Any person desiring to address the Commission shall file a written request with the city
             clerk prior to consideration of the matter by the Commission or prior to the public
             comment portion of a meeting. The person wishing to speak shall complete a comment
             card for each agenda item the person wishes to address, which shall include the
             person's full name, address, and the numbered agenda item. The person will not be
             recognized if the comment card is not completed.

             REVENUE AND EXPENSE FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING ON
             OCTOBER 1, 2026, AND ENDING ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2027,
             APPROPRIATING THE USE OF FUNDS SHOWN THEREIN AS MAY BE
             NEEDED OR DEEMED NECESSARY TO DEFRAY ALL EXPENSES AND
             LIABILITIES OF THE CITY FOR SUCH FISCAL YEAR; PROVIDING A
             SAVINGS CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR
             OTHER PURPOSES.
             Staff Recommended Motion:
             (Resolution No. 210-26(F) setting the tentative millage rate must be
             considered first, and Resolution No. 211-26(F) adopting a tentative budget
             will be considered after the millage rate resolution. The Resolutions must
             be considered separately.)

             Approve Resolution No. 210-26(F) adopting a tentative ad valorem millage
             rate of 8.0803 mills for general operating purposes, which is 5.05 percent
             more than the calculated rolled-back rate of 7.6915, and a millage rate of
             0.0533 mills for general obligation debt service, for a total millage rate of
             8.1336 mills; and

             Approve Resolution No. 211-26(F) adopting a tentative budget for fiscal
             year 2026/2027.


            Background Information:
            Section 200.065, Florida Statutes, requires among other things, that the
            City Commission of the City of West Palm Beach, Florida adopt tentative
            millage rates necessary to fund the tentative budget, after notice and public
            hearing, prior to adopting a final budget of final millage rates.

            Resolution No. 210-26(F) adopts the FY 2026/27 tentative ad valorem
            rates of 8.0803 mills for operating purposes and 0.0533 mills for debt
            service on voter-approved general obligation debt for an aggregate millage
            of 8.1336. The tentative operating millage rate of 8.0803 mills is 5.05%
            above the calculated rolled back rate of 7.6915.

            Resolution No. 211-26(F) adopts the tentative estimates of revenue and
            expenditures for FY 2026/2027. The total Citywide budget for all funds
            (excluding CRA and ECR) is $949,433,520. However, the operating
            millage mentioned previously relates only to the General Fund budget,
            which totals $302,725,831. The budget is balanced on the tentative millage
            rates discussed above.

            Public Hearings: Section 200.065 Florida Statutes requires that a taxing
            authority hold two (2) Public Hearings to adopt the annual budget.

            At this first Public Hearing, the City Commission will first hear public
            comment on the proposed millage rates and budget for FY 2026/2027.
            After hearing the public comment, the Commission may discuss the FY
            2026/2027 budget and make changes. The Commission will then adopt the
            tentative millage rates through Resolution No. 210-26(F) and then adopt
            the tentative budget for FY 2026/2027 through Resolution No. 211-26(F).

            A second Public Hearing to adopt the final millage rates and final budget
            for FY 2026/2027 is scheduled for September 23, 2026, at 5:01 PM and
            will be noticed in the newspaper.

            Fiscal Note:
            These items set the tentative budget and millage rate.

## 4. COMMENTS FROM THE PUBLIC


      Public comments are limited to 3 (three) minutes. Anyone wishing to address the Commission
      should complete a "Comments by the Public" card and present it to the City Clerk prior to the
      Public Comments. When you are called to speak, please go to the podium and state your name
      and address for the record prior to addressing Commission. The Commission will not discuss
      the matter nor respond to the comment this evening. Comments made will become part of the
      record and may be addressed at a later date.
