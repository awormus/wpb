## 2. CIVILITY AND DECORUM

The City of West Palm Beach is committed to civility and decorum by its officials, employees and members of the public who attend this meeting. The City Code, Secs. 2-31(8), 2-31(18) and 2-31(22), provides in pertinent part:
- Officials shall be recognized by the Chair and shall not interrupt a speaker.
- Public comment shall be addressed to the City Commission as a whole and not to any individual on the dais or in the audience.
- Displays of anger, rudeness, ridicule, impatience, lack of respect and personal attacks are strictly prohibited.
- Unauthorized remarks from the audience, stamping of feet, whistles, yells and similar demonstrations shall not be permitted.
- Offenders may be removed from the meeting.
- Any person desiring to address the Commission shall file a written request with the city clerk prior to consideration of the matter by the Commission or prior to the public comment portion of a meeting. The person wishing to speak shall complete a comment card for each agenda item the person wishes to address, which shall include the person's full name, address, and the numbered agenda item. The person will not be recognized if the comment card is not completed.

Finance

#### Ordinance/Resolution


> RESOLUTION NO. 216-24(F): A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA ADOPTING FINAL AD VALOREM MILLAGE RATES OF 8.1308 MILLS FOR GENERAL OPERATING PURPOSES, WHICH IS 3.19 PERCENT MORE THAN THE CALCULATED “ROLLEDBACK RATE” OF 7.8796 AND 0.0633 MILLS FOR GENERAL OBLIGATION DEBT SERVICE FOR A TOTAL OF 8.1941 MILLS AND LEVYING AD VALOREM TAXES FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2024, AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2025; SETTING FORTH CERTAIN INFORMATION REGARDING “ROLLED-BACK RATES”; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

> RESOLUTION NO. 217-24(F): A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA ADOPTING FINAL ESTIMATES OF REVENUE AND EXPENSE FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING ON OCTOBER 1, 2024, AND ENDING ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2025, APPROPRIATING THE USE OF FUNDS SHOWN THEREIN AS MAY BE NEEDED OR DEEMED NECESSARY TO DEFRAY ALL EXPENSES AND LIABILITIES OF THE CITY FOR SUCH FISCAL YEAR; PROVIDING A SAVINGS CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

#### Staff Recommended Motion

Resolution No. 216-24(F) setting the final millage rate must be considered first, and Resolution No. 217-24(F) adopting a final budget will be considered after the millage rate resolution. The Resolutions must be considered separately.

Resolution No. 216-24(F) Motion: I move approval of Resolution No. 21624(F) adopting a final ad valorem millage rate of 8.1308 mills for general operating purposes, which is 3.19 percent more than the calculated rolled-back rate of 7.8796 and a millage rate of 0.0633 mills for general obligation debt service, for a total millage rate of 8.1941 mills.

Resolution No. 217-24(F) Motion: I move approval of Resolution No. 21724(F) adopting a final budget for fiscal year 2024/2025.


Special City Commission - Sep 25 2024

#### Background Information

Section 200.065 Florida Statutes requires that a taxing authority hold two (2) public hearings to adopt the annual operating budget. At the First Public Hearing held on September 9, 2024, the City Commission commented on the budget and then adopted a tentative millage rate and tentative operating budget.

On September 9, 2024, the first public hearing was held, and the City Commission approved the tentative millage rates and operating budget.

The second public hearing, on September 25, 2024, was noticed by publication in the Palm Beach Post on September 22, 2024. The City Commission will hear public comment on the millage rates and the budget and may make revisions prior to final adoption of the millage rates and the budget for the fiscal year 2024/2025.

Resolution No. 216-24(F) adopts the FY 2024/2025 final ad valorem millage rates of 8.1308 for operating purposes and 0.0633 mills for debt service on voter approved general obligation debt for an aggregate millage rate of 8.1941. The operating millage is 3.19% above the “rolledback rate” of 7.8796.

Resolution No. 217-24(F) adopts the final estimates of revenue and expenses for the fiscal year commencing on October 1, 2024, and ending on September 30, 2025, as its annual budget, appropriating the use of funds therein as may be needed or deemed necessary to defray all expenses and liabilities of the City for such fiscal year.

The total City-wide budget for all funds is $769,900,134 (excluding the CRA and ECR) for the fiscal year 2024/2025. The operating millage mentioned previously relates only to the General Fund budget, which totals $264,019,397. The budget is balanced on the millage rates discussed above.

#### Fiscal Note

Approval will authorize budget appropriations for FY2025 Revenue and Expenditures.
