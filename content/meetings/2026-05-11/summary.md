# Summary — 2026-05-11 regular meeting

Outcomes below are from the official **pass/fail** sheet (not minutes). Agenda text is in `agenda.md`.

## Outcomes (pass/fail)

- **Agenda changes:** none.
- **Proclamations / presentation:** presented (Peace Officers Memorial Day / Police Week; National Public Works Week; Mental Health Awareness Month; Palm Beach County Commission on Ethics overview).
- **[8.1 Receive & file](./agenda.md#item-8-1):** 2026 Disparity Study (Griffin & Strong, March 2026) received and filed.
- **Consent:** [all items approved](./agenda.md#item-9), including March 2/16/30 minutes; **[Res. 96-26(F)](./agenda.md#item-9-4)** ($70,739 State Law Enforcement Forfeiture Receipts for LRAD, Simunition, and ballistic equipment); **[Res. 107-26 / 106-26(F)](./agenda.md#item-9-5)** (FDOT LAP Supplemental Agreement for **49th Street** Traffic Calming and Bicycle Improvements, **$1,172,608**, plus grant/match appropriations); **[Res. 109–112-26](./agenda.md#item-9-6)** (employee medical/dental/life/disability/vision and voluntary benefits).
- **[Res. 102-26](./agenda.md#item-10-1):** approved — recognizes **Northwood Marina / NOMAR** neighborhood (≈40th–45th Streets, Broadway to N. Flagler, plus Marina CPD east of Flagler).
- **[Res. 98-26](./agenda.md#item-11-1) (quasi-judicial):** approved — vacate/abandon **13,645 sq ft** T-shaped alley (N. Railroad / 10th / N. Dixie / 11th) for NORA Phase 3; city compensation **$680,000**.
