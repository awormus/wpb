# Regular City Commission Meeting — November 9, 2026

**When:** November 9, 2026, 5:00 PM  
**Where:** Commission Chambers, 401 Clematis Street  
**Source:** Official final agenda PDF on wpb.org (linked below). This page summarizes **what is scheduled**, not vote outcomes.

**Final agenda replaces the November 2 draft.** Versus the draft, public hearing item 8.2 (Ordinance No. 5190-26, Breakwater Hospitality Group ground lease for a Currie Park restaurant) is **not on the final agenda**. Other numbered items and titles match the draft. Items 9.1 (Ordinance No. 5183-26) and 9.2 (Resolution No. 217-26), the CityPlace requests, remain the two quasi-judicial items the October 26, 2026 pass/fail agenda listed as continued to this meeting ([Oct 26 pass/fail](../2026-10-26/pass-fail.md#item-8)).

## Opening

Call to order, moment of silence, pledge, civility/decorum, and any agenda additions/deletions/reorganization (see full text for exact wording).

## Consent Calendar ([6](./agenda.md#item-6))

- **[6.1](./agenda.md#item-6-1):** Minutes of the September 8, 2026, Special City Commission Meeting.
- **[6.2](./agenda.md#item-6-2):** Minutes of the September 14, 2026, City Commission Meeting.
- **[6.3](./agenda.md#item-6-3):** Resolution No. 239-26 accepting a $15,000 FDOT litter-prevention grant for Keep West Palm Beach Beautiful; Resolution No. 238-26(F) appropriating the funds.
- **[6.4](./agenda.md#item-6-4):** Resolution No. 248-26(F) appropriating $146,000 from Federal Law Enforcement Forfeiture Receipts for investigative operating expenses.

- **[6.6](./agenda.md#item-6-6):** Resolution No. 254-26(F) year-end budget clean-up amendments for FY 2025/2026.

- **[7.2](./agenda.md#item-7-2):** Resolution No. 251-26 adopting the 2026 Watershed Master Plan (CRS Class 4 prerequisite; FEMA CRS recertification audit November 13, 2026).
