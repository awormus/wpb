## 4. CIVILITY AND DECORUM

The City of West Palm Beach is committed to civility and decorum by its officials, employees and members of the public who attend this meeting. The City Code, Secs. 2-31(8), 2-31(18) and 2-31(22), provides in pertinent part:
- Officials shall be recognized by the Chair and shall not interrupt a speaker.
- Public comment shall be addressed to the City Commission as a whole and not to any individual on the dais or in the audience.
- Displays of anger, rudeness, ridicule, impatience, lack of respect and personal attacks are strictly prohibited.
- Unauthorized remarks from the audience, stamping of feet, whistles, yells and similar demonstrations shall not be permitted.
- Offenders may be removed from the meeting.
- Any person desiring to address the Commission shall file a written request with the city clerk prior to consideration of the matter by the Commission or prior to the public comment portion of a meeting. The person wishing to speak shall complete a comment card for each agenda item the person wishes to address, which shall include the person's full name, address, and the numbered agenda item. > RESOLUTION NO. 165-25(F): A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATION OR TRANSFER OF FUNDS IN FISCAL YEAR 2024/2025 IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 4.03 OF THE CITY CHARTER OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, FOR THE PURPOSE OF AMENDING THE GRANT CAPITAL PROJECT FUND BUDGET TO PROVIDE FOR THE RECEIPT OF A STATE OF FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION STATEFUNDED GRANT, AND APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE TRAFFIC SIGNAL HARDENING PROJECT; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

#### Background Information

In 2023, the City coordinated with Palm Beach County about the conditions of the traffic signals in several downtown locations. The County identified several intersections where they recommend replacement of the existing signal pole structures because of damage caused by the aging of the poles.

City staff were concerned about four locations in particular: at the intersections of Clematis Street and Olive Avenue; Datura Street and Olive Avenue; Banyan Boulevard and Flagler Drive; and 3rd Street and Flagler Drive that need replacement in the near future because the traffic signal posts were installed prior to 1980 and need to be updated to meet current wind load requirements to prevent damage to signals and hazards


City Commission - Jun 23 2025

to traffic. Replacement of these signals will prevent possible damage to and disruption of service to traffic signals at the four intersections during windstorm events.

In late 2023, City staff met with the City’s State Legislature lobbyist team, and it was identified that a project for hardening the traffic signals would be strongly considered for approval by the Legislature and Governor for state appropriation funding.

The City then pursued a request for State Appropriations funding in early 2024, asking for $475,000 in funding to help pay for the hardening upgrade of the traffic signals at these four (4) intersections.

In June 2024, the City was notified that Governor DeSantis signed the 2024-2025 Fiscal Year budget wherein the requested Traffic Signal Hardening funding was included in the General Appropriations Act.

In August 2024, FDOT notified the City of the award of the funding and that it would be administered through FDOT.

In May 2025, the State Funded Grant agreement was finalized by FDOT and provided to the City for execution.

The total construction cost estimate for this project is $905,152. The City’s match for this will be $430,152.

The work is expected to start in the fall of 2025 and be completed by June 2026.

Resolution No. 164-25 authorizes the Mayor to execute the grant agreement, and Resolution No. 165-25(F) appropriates the funding.

3: Commissioner Christy Fox.

#### Ordinance/Resolution


> ORDINANCE NO. 5135-25: AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, AMENDING THE CODE OF ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, AT CHAPTER 86 – TRAFFIC AND PARKING, ARTICLE IV – STOPPING, STANDING AND PARKING, DIVISION I – GENERALLY, TO DELETE SECTION 86-245 (CONSTRUCTION PROJECT PARKING) AND AMEND SECTION 86-246 (CONSTRUCTION TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT PLAN) TO REVISE PLAN REQUIREMENTS AND APPROVAL; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE, A CODIFICATION CLAUSE, AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

> RESOLUTION NO. 158-25: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, AMENDING THE COMPREHENSIVE FEE SCHEDULE TO PROVIDE A FEE FOR REVIEW AND ADMINISTRATION OF THE CONSTRUCTION TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT AND PARKING PERMIT PROGRAM; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

#### Staff Recommended Motion

Approve Ordinance No. 5135-25 and Resolution No. 158-25.

#### Background Information

The City of West Palm Beach is undergoing significant development with many projects ongoing throughout the City.

Many residential neighborhoods have been impacted by increased demand for on-street parking within residential neighborhoods due to construction and commercial activities.

City Administration and staff from Engineering, Parking, and Development Services have been meeting to develop and implement various measures to address the situation.

As one measure, the City desires to amend the regulations regarding construction traffic and parking plans.


City Commission - Jun 23 2025

Ordinance No. 5135-25 amends Section 86-246 of the City Code to require Developers, contractors, and/or property owners to obtain a construction traffic management and parking permit.

In order to obtain a permit, the proposed plan for the construction vehicle route for delivery of materials, staging area, and a parking plan for construction workers and construction vehicles. Parking plans may require approval from the City’s parking administrator. The construction traffic management and parking permit will be reviewed for approval by the Engineering Services Department and must be issued prior to issuance of a building permit.

Upon the issuance of three (3) violations for non-compliance of an approved construction traffic and parking management plan, the building official shall issue a stop-work order until a revised plan is approved.

Resolution No. 158-25 amends the City Comprehensive Fee Schedule to establish the permit fee of $300 for up to two (2) reviews of the application for a Construction Traffic Management and Parking Permit.

### 8.1. Appeal of the Historic Preservation Board's decision in Case # 25-02, a request by Jack Rice Roach of Gunster Law on behalf of 418 36th Street Realty LLC (Emily Gutierrez) for the relocation of an existing contributing structure within the lot.

#### Originating Department

Development Services

#### Staff Recommended Motion

Approve a Certificate of Appropriateness to allow the relocation of an existing contributing structure approximately thirty (30) feet west of its existing location at 426 36th Street.

This motion is based on the factual testimony presented and the staff report, which constitute competent, substantial evidence, along with the finding that the relocations meets the criteria set forth in the Secretary of Interior Standards for Rehabilitation found in Section 94-49(c)(3) and complies with the Relocation Standards in Section 94-49(c)(4)(c) of the Zoning and Land Development Regulations.

#### Background Information

The property at 426 36th Street includes a 1,846 square foot contributing


City Commission - Jun 23 2025

structure known as the Dr. Daniel & Elizabeth Macintosh House, built in a Mission Revival style in 1928. The 426 36th Street property has functionally operated with the adjacent property on the east (422 36th Street), which previously contained a pool and a gazebo. The pool, gazebo, and an accessory structure were approved for demolition by the Historic Preservation Board (HPB) last December 2024.

On March 25, 2025, the HPB heard an application by Russell Rosicki of Design R Architects FL LLP on behalf of the current property owner for a Certificate of Appropriateness requesting the relocation of the existing contributing structure thirty (30) feet towards the west to allow for a minor lot split of the two (2) combined properties into two (2) different lots.
