# Summary — 2026-07-20 regular meeting

Outcomes below are from the official **pass/fail** sheet (not minutes). Agenda text is in `agenda.md`.

## Outcomes (pass/fail)

- **Proclamations:** presented (Parks & Recreation Month; PBSC America 250 Day).
- **Appointments / Consent:** all approved (incl. railroad crossing reimbursement agreements; FRA CRISI grant application Res. 158-26).
- **[Res. 161-26](./agenda.md#item-10-1):** approved — Term Sheet with **Pine Crest Preparatory School** for sale of ~57 acres (south of Roebuck Rd / Turnpike) for a K–12 school.
- **[Res. 162-26](./agenda.md#item-10-2):** approved — Howard Park Community Center construction contract with Shiff Construction (**$7,549,398.15**).
- **[Res. 172-26](./agenda.md#item-10-3):** approved — Dreher Trail North reconfiguration (Cox Science Center expansion).
- **[Res. 166-26](./agenda.md#item-10-4):** approved — **$640,000** funding commitment for Southridge Phase II LIHTC application (3801 Georgia Ave).
- **[Res. 173-26](./agenda.md#item-10-5):** approved — affordable/workforce housing agreement & restrictive covenant for **District Pointe** (1501 Belvedere Rd).
- **[Res. 134-26](./agenda.md#item-11-1):** approved — accept HUD entitlement grants (**$5,223,401.94** total) and FY2026–27 Annual Action Plan.
- **[Ord. 5177-26](./agenda.md#item-11-2) (2nd reading):** approved — Flagler/Washington MF32 Zoning in Progress (6 months).
- **[Ords. 5174-26 / 5175-26 / 5176-26](./agenda.md#item-11-3) (2nd reading):** approved — 5710 N. Haverhill annexation + FLUM + MF32 zoning.
- **[Res. 136-26](./agenda.md#item-11-4):** approved — Special Site Plan Review for 318-unit multifamily at 5710 N. Haverhill (44 affordable/workforce units; Kolter / Palm Lake Baptist).
