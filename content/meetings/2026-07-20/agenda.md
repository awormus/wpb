### 9.2. Resolution No. 148-26 approves an interlocal agreement between the City of West Palm Beach and Saint Lucie County to share public safety radio interoperability.

#### Originating Department

Police

#### Ordinance/Resolution


> RESOLUTION NO. 148-26: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH AND ST. LUCIE COUNTY FOR SHARED USE OF PUBLIC SAFETY RADIO COMMUNICATIONS INFRASTRUCTURE; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND OTHER PURPOSES.

#### Background Information

The West Palm Beach Police Department (WPBPD) has several interlocal agreements with local counties and municipalities granting access to their respective public safety radio communication networks. The interlocal agreements grant permission to program WPBPD public safety radio devices with the outside agencies' public safety radio network.


Multi-county and regional radio interoperability ensures seamless, realtime communication across different public safety agencies and jurisdictions. It directly eliminates communication bottlenecks during largescale crises, mutual aid deployments, and task force operations, ultimately boosting response coordination and saving lives.
The most critical benefits include:
- Improved Crisis Response: During large-scale emergencies that span multiple counties (such as hurricanes, wildfires, or widespread severe weather), regional interoperability establishes a shared operating picture, allowing police, fire, and EMS to coordinate efforts and avoid delays.

- Optimal Resource Allocation: When a local emergency outpaces an individual county's resources, interoperability makes it much easier to request and direct mutual aid, as dispatchers can track and route resources from neighboring jurisdictions.

- Enhanced Situational Awareness: First responders can exchange critical information across agencies without relying on manual relays through dispatch. This means real-time sharing of incident details, suspect descriptions, and hazard warnings. Mutual Backup Redundancy: If one local master dispatch site or communications tower goes offline during a disaster, a broader regional network ensures that public safety communications can be rerouted through neighboring infrastructure without losing contact.


- Streamlined Data Exchange: Many modern regional radio systems also bridge data, such as GPS locations and computer-aided dispatch (CAD) information, further aligning multi-agency responses.

The interlocal agreement has no fiscal impact and is not district-specific.

Resolution No. 148-26 approves the interlocal agreement with Saint Lucie County.

#### Fiscal Note

No fiscal impact.

#### Background Information

The Florida Department of Transportation (FDOT) will be undertaking the State Road No. 5/US-1 Broadway Avenue Improvements project from 25th Street to 45th Street (“FDOT Project"). The FDOT Project involves milling and resurfacing, sidewalk and driveway replacement, drainage improvements, lighting, and signalization improvements. Within the 1-mile length of the FDOT Project corridor, incidental improvements to the City's existing utilities will be required (Utility Work). Accordingly, the City’s related utility work will be competitively bid as part of the FDOT project and constructed by the FDOT Contractor.

Under the terms of the Agreement, the City shall reimburse FDOT a total estimated amount of $127,456 for the cost of the Utility Work. This amount includes the cost of utility items plus ten percent (10%) construction contingency and two percent (2%) for the CEI (Construction Engineering & Inspection).


Resolution No. 150-26 approves the UWHCA with FDOT for the City’s incidental utility adjustments within State Road No. 5/US-1 Broadway Avenue Improvements project from 25th Street to 45th Street.

### 10.1. Resolution No. 161-26 approving a Term Sheet with Pine Crest Preparatory School for the sale of approximately 57 acres of City property for the development of a school.

#### Originating Department

City Attorney's Office

#### Ordinance/Resolution


> RESOLUTION NO. 161-26: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA APPROVING A TERM SHEET WITH PINE CREST PREPATORY SCHOOL, INC., FOR THE SALE OF APPROXIMATELY 57 ACRES LOCATED SOUTH OF ROEBUCK ROAD AND ADJACENT OT THE FLORIDA TURNPIKE FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SCHOOL; WAIVING THE TIMING REQUIREMENT FOR THE APPRAISALS; AUTHORIZING ACCESS TO THE PROPERTY TO PERFORM DUE DILIGENCE INSPECTIONS; AUTHORIZING OWNER’S CONSENT FOR SUBMITTAL OF APPLICATIONS FOR LAND DEVELOPMENT APPROVALS; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

#### Staff Recommended Motion

Approve Resolution No. 161-26.

#### Background Information

Pine Crest Preparatory School, Inc. (“Pine Crest”), desire to develop, construct and contract for the operation of a school for students in kindergarten through grade 12, as such grades may be phased in, within the City.

Pine Crest desires to purchase from the City approximately 57 acres of the southern portion of City-owned property South of Roebuck Road and East of the Florida Turnpike (the “Property”). The adjacent northern City property contains a well field utilized by the City’s Public Utilities Department for water supply. By Resolution No. 54-25, the City Commission determined that the Property is not needed for City purposes and declared the Property to be surplus.

The City and Pine Crest have negotiated a Term Sheet establishing the material terms for the sale of the Property.


Section 2-31(27)(c) of the Code of Ordinances requires that two (2) appraisals be obtained for the Property, and that the price shall not be less than 85 percent (85%) of the average of the two (2) appraisals. The Code further requires that the appraisals not be earlier than six (6) months before the first reading of the ordinance approving the conveyance agreement, yet allows waiver of this provision by unanimous vote of the membership of the City Commission.

Two (2) appraisals were obtained in 2025 with an average appraised value of $6,625,000. By Resolution No. 54-25, the City Commission directed that more recent appraisals be obtained, but the City Commission would not consider a purchase price less than $6,625,000. Two (2) more recent appraisals were obtained, which considered various factors that reduced the appraised values.

The purchase price under the Pine Crest Term Sheet is based on the $6,625,000 value.

To promote access to quality education, Pine Crest provides financial aid to qualified students based on demonstrated financial need. Families with household incomes below the median for West Palm Beach would be expected to qualify for significant tuition assistance, up to 99% of tuition.

Pine Crest financial aid is need-based and customized to an individual family's financial profile. Families receiving tuition assistance at a level of 51% or greater will be eligible for inclusion aid. Inclusion aid, designed to enhance the overall experience and access to extracurriculars, covers expenses such as laptop computers, extended-day programs, field trips, enrichment, academic assistance, and transportation, when applicable. In addition, qualified West Palm Beach residents will also receive:
- Waived application fee;

- Early preferred admission deadline and expedited admission decision notification;

- Priority review and processing for financial aid; and

- Access to targeted financial assistance available to qualified West Palm Beach residents.


The Term Sheet requires Pine Crest to provide the following additional community benefits: Contractors & Subcontractors: The School shall engage contractors and subcontractors from small businesses certified by the City at a value of not less than eighteen percent (18%) of the total construction costs.

Water Pressure: The School shall contribute an amount not to exceed $1,500,000 toward the costs of a pump station/water facility to enhance the water pressure to the intended school and surrounding community.

Pine Crest also seeks authorization from the City to perform due-diligence investigations while the Purchase and Sale Agreement is being drafted.

The Purchase and Sale Agreement will be brought back to the City Commission for approval.

The City has an interest in promoting access to quality education provided by both public and private schools. The City Commission may designate that the proceeds from this sale of City land may be used for the purposes of enhancing educational opportunities at Roosevelt Elementary School.

Resolution No. 161-26 approves the Term Sheet with Pine Crest Preparatory School, Inc.
