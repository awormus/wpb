# Summary — 2026-04-27 regular meeting

Outcomes below are from the official **pass/fail** sheet (not minutes). Agenda text is in `agenda.md`.

## Outcomes (pass/fail)

- **Agenda changes:** none.
- **Proclamation:** National Small Business Week (May 4–8, 2026) presented.
- **Consent:** [all items approved](./agenda.md#item-7), including **[Res. 65-26(F)](./agenda.md#item-7-1)** ($79,852 State Aid to Libraries); **[Res. 76-26(F)](./agenda.md#item-7-2)** ($200,000 Water/Sewer Construction Fund for 24th Street sanitary/water improvements); **[Res. 84-26](./agenda.md#item-7-3)** ($100,000 Highsmith settlement); **[Res. 85-26](./agenda.md#item-7-4)** (utility liens **$19,795.09** for Jan 2026); **[Res. 79-26](./agenda.md#item-7-5)** (abandon 5-ft utility easement at 124 Churchill Rd); **[Res. 93-26](./agenda.md#item-7-6)** (PBC EMS grant interlocal); **[Res. 95-26](./agenda.md#item-7-7)** (Kidde in-kind donation — 1,000 smoke/CO alarms, ~$59,970 retail); **[Res. 94-26](./agenda.md#item-7-8)** (elderly nutrition services at Gloria Y. Williams Multicultural Center / PBC facility agreement).
- **[Res. 100-26](./agenda.md#item-8-1):** approved — NOMAR neighborhood utility coordination (40th–44th St, Broadway to N. Flagler) with Huizenga Holdings; City cost share NTE **$3,763,046** (≈40%).
