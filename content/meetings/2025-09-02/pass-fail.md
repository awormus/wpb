#### Originating Department

Public Utilities

#### Ordinance/Resolution


> RESOLUTION NO. 183-25: A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA AUTHORIZING AND APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND THE PALM BEACH SOIL AND WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICT TO PROVIDE IRRIGATION EVALUATIONS AND WATER CONSERVATION SERVICES UNDER THE URBAN IRRIGATION EFFICIENCY IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

#### Background Information

The City of West Palm Beach and the Palm Beach Soil and Water Conservation District, through an interlocal agreement, operate the Urban Irrigation Efficiency Program, providing free comprehensive irrigation system evaluations and water conservation audits. The free audits are made available to 100 residents this fiscal year, and may be renewed in subsequent years pending budget allocation.

This new agreement modifies the original pilot program, with the removal of a provision that offered grant-funded modifications to customer irrigation systems, based upon the audit results. This revision follows the end of the grant that covered that expense.

The program provides recommendations to our customers for a more efficient irrigation system to reduce their potable water use. The program strives to educate residents, land users, and businesses on ways to protect our natural resources, promote best management practices, and conserve, improve, and sustain the environment both economically and efficiently to the benefit of both the City and the District.

As part of a long-term strategic effort to stimulate revitalization within the north end of the City, Huizenga Holdings Inc. has acquired the majority of the lots within the area located east of Broadway, west of North Flagler Drive, north of 40th Street, and south of 45th Street. The vision for the first phase of development is to serve as

The proposed text amendment will revise as well as reduce the dimensional requirements relating to the minimum lot dimensions; the minimum front, side, and rear setbacks for a principal building; the required landscaped area within the front yard; and the maximum driveway width. The amendment applies to all lots within the subject area and will allow for the construction of larger structures on each lot with the reduced setbacks.
The main requests include:

Minimum Setbacks The applicant is requesting the following changes to the setbacks:
- Front Setback: A reduction from 25 feet to 18 feet

> ORDINANCE NO. 5139-25: AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, AMENDING CHAPTER 94, ARTICLE II, SECTIONS 94-36, 94-38, AND TO CREATE SECTION 94-57; ARTICLE VII, AND SECTION 94-236; AND AMENDING ARTICLE XIX, SECTION 94-611; TO CREATE AND ESTABLISH DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS FOR THE NORTH FLAGLER OVERLAY DISTRICT; DECLARING THIS PROPOSED ZONING TEXT AMENDMENT TO BE CONSISTENT WITH THE COMPREHENSIVE PLAN AND THE ZONING AND LAND DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS OF THE CITY; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

#### Staff Recommended Motion

Approve Ordinance No. 5139-25 amending the City's Zoning and Land Development Regulations to create the North Flagler Overlay District and zoning regulations for the district.

This motion is based upon the factual testimony presented, the staff report, the recommendation of the Planning Board, and the findings that the proposed amendment complies with all the amendment standards found in Section 94-32 of the City's Zoning and Land Development Regulations.

#### Background Information

Since 2021 and the COVID migration, there has been a renewed interest and an increase in development activity on waterfront properties within the North Flagler Drive corridor. As a result, the public has raised concerns, including, but not limited to, the impact of new high-rise buildings adjacent to existing single-family residences and across the street from the existing Northwood Harbor Historic District.

Since 2024, several meetings have been conducted with the community to receive input and present alternative proposed regulations to address the community concerns.

The result of the community engagement is the proposed North Flagler Overlay District (NOFOD), whose intent is to create a place of common vision and physical predictability for new construction along the waterfront; to reinforce the existing architectural character within the subject area; and to provide for development projects that are complementary to the surrounding residential neighborhood. This can be achieved by maintaining the general character of the existing zoning districts within the subject area, while superimposing and establishing


City Commission - Sep 02 2025

regulatory parameters that provide a predictable environment for all new projects. The specific regulations include: 1. Maximum Floor Area Ratios depending on the parcel frontage along Flagler Drive. 2. Conditional front setbacks depending on the building height. 3. Conditional side setbacks for the podium and tower.

Additional height above the permitted by right under the MF32 zoning regulations may be achieved through an incentive program which: 1. Allows additional height up to 300 feet for properties with a lot area equal to or larger than 2.5 acres. 2. Allows additional height up to 240 feet for properties with a lot area of less than 2.5 acres. 3. A monetary contribution of $10.98 per square foot of gross building area above the permitted by right will be assessed for infrastructure improvements within the area, or the Developer can construct the improvements and obtain credit. 4. Infrastructure improvements will be located within the area between Broadway, the Intracoastal Waterway, 36th Street, and the northern City limits.
