### 2.1. Swearing in ceremony for newly re-elected City Commissioners for the City of West Palm Beach.

#### Originating Department

Mayor's Office

#### Background Information

The City's Charter requires that after each annual election, the newly elected members be inducted and assume office at a public meeting.

This swearing-in ceremony is being held for the purpose of inducting the newly elected members whose office will commence on April 4, 2025, pursuant to Sec. 2-1, of the City of West Palm Beach Code of Ordinances, which provides the term of office of a newly elected city commissioner shall commence at 5:00 p.m. on the fourth Thursday immediately following the regular election. The oath of office will be administered to the newly re-elected City Commissioners: Shalonda Warren (District 2) and Joseph Peduzzi (District 4).


Special City Commission - Mar 31 2025

<a id="item-2-2"></a>
### 2.2. Election of the President of the City Commission.

#### Originating Department

Mayor's Office

#### Background Information

The election of the President of the City Commission will take place pursuant to Sec. 2.05 of the City's Charter which reads: "Sec. 2.05. Induction and meetings. After each annual city election, the newly elected members shall be inducted and assume office at a public meeting. At such meeting, the city commissioners shall elect one (1) of their number to the office of the president of the city commission who shall serve at the pleasure of the city commission."
