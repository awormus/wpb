# FY2027 budget & millage

**Categories:** `budget`

## What this is

Florida **Truth in Millage (TRIM)** rules (**§200.065, F.S.**) require the City to set maximum millage, then hold two public hearings to adopt tentative and final operating millage and the annual budget. In 2026 the Commission used special meetings for: (1) **Jul 16** — set not-to-exceed / maximum rates (agenda cites maximum operating millage **8.1308** and debt **0.0533**); (2) **Sep 8** — tentative millage/budget (agenda notices aggregate millage **8.1336**); (3) **Sep 23** — final millage/budget as noticed on the special agenda.

## Why it matters for residents

Millage rates determine the property-tax levy funding general city services. Special hearings are the formal TRIM checkpoints; regular meetings may reference budget items but the special agendas carry the adoption votes described above.
