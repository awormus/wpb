# Norton / Pioneer Park lease (Ord. 5167-26)

Ordinance authorizing a lease of City-owned **Pioneer Park / Norton Park** (green median at **1450 S. Olive Avenue**, between S. Flagler Drive, Pioneer Plaza, Diana Place, and Acteon Place) to the **Norton Museum of Art, Inc.** for development and operation as a public art / cultural park that remains open to the public.

Material terms on the agenda/pass-fail extracts include a **50-year** term with a **20-year** renewal option; base rent **$100/year** (with contingent additional rent after year 10); Norton authority over artworks and naming (subject to limitations); bollard/traffic-control rights on Diana and Actaeon Places for certain Friday–Saturday windows; community education programs; local-workforce and small-business participation targets; and construction completion deadlines.
