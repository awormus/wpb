# Forest Hill High — Winters Street vacation

**Categories:** `land-use`, `education`

## What this is

**Res. 208-26** is a School District request (agenda names Joyell Shaw and Simone …) to vacate/abandon an approximately **50×100 ft** portion of **Winters Street** (~5,000 sq ft) east of the school area so parcels can be consolidated for an **87-space off-site parking lot** serving Forest Hill Community High School. Agenda background cites a companion **ZBA Class B Special Use (PB 3560)** approved 4–0 on **2026-09-03** with a condition that Commission approve the abandonment, and notes an earlier 2010 special use that expired without construction.

## Why it matters for residents

Street vacation permanently changes the public right-of-way. Neighbors and school families may care about parking, access, and whether the ROW remains public. Outcome is not invented here.
