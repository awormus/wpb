# Greene School Commercial Planned Development (Ord. 5169-26 / Res. 103-26)

Rezoning of approximately **5.38 acres** at **2001 South Dixie Highway** from General Commercial (GC) to Commercial Planned Development (CPD) for The Greene School, with companion development-regulations resolution. Applicants (agenda): Jon Schmidt and Christopher Claeyesen of Schmidt Nichols, on behalf of Tobias Partners LP and Jeff Greene.
