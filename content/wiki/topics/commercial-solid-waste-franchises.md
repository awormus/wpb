# Commercial solid-waste collection franchises

**Categories:** `utilities`

## What this is

Ordinance No. **5184-26** amends **Chapter 74, Article V** covering franchises for commercial solid-waste collection. Agenda background says the revisions follow **Ord. 5133-25** (May 2025), which shifted fees toward revenue-based calculation, and proposes clarifying franchise provisions—including a proposed cap of **ten** active franchises and clearer fee-calculation language.

## Why it matters for residents

Commercial haulers and property owners operate under city franchise rules and fees. Clarifying how many franchises may be active and how fees are calculated affects competition and costs for commercial collection. Details below are from the agenda packet only.
