# Affordable housing inventory & surplus conveyances

**Categories:** `housing`, `land-use`

## What this is

Florida law (**§166.0451, F.S.**) requires municipalities to prepare an inventory of real property suitable for affordable housing on a three-year cycle. Separately, the Commission has considered **surplus determinations** and **conveyances** of city/CRA parcels toward affordable or workforce housing projects (Broadway corridor, Tamarind Avenue, Sapodilla strip, and related term sheets).

## Why it matters for residents

Inventory adoption does **not** itself sell or lease land—it flags properties the city has identified as suitable. Surplus and conveyance votes are the steps that can move specific parcels. Tracking both helps citizens see the statutory list and the project-specific deals on the same timeline.
