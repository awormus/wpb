# FPU natural-gas franchise (Ord. 5148-25)

**Categories:** `utilities`

## What this is

Ordinance No. **5148-25** grants **Florida Public Utilities Company** a **non-exclusive 30-year** franchise to distribute natural gas in city rights-of-way, with a **6%** franchise fee on gross natural-gas sales revenue in the City (per agenda/pass-fail text). Agenda background notes the prior framework under **Ord. 2319-89** (approved Sep 18, 1989) and that the City controls its rights-of-way.

## Why it matters for residents

Franchise terms set how a private utility uses public ROW and what fee the city receives. This ordinance took multiple readings—including a “not approved” second reading—before later approval.
