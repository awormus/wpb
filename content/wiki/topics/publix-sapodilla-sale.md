# Publix Sapodilla sale (Ord. 5166-26)

Ordinance authorizing sale of City-owned property at **202–206 N. Sapodilla Avenue** to **Publix Super Markets, Inc.** for development of a full-service supermarket, purchase price **$3,526,684**, with a purchase and sale agreement. Sale ordinance requires super-majority (4/5) approval at either first or second reading (Code §22-31(27)).
