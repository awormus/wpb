# Axon Enterprise — police technology contracts

**Categories:** `public-safety`, `budget`

## What this is

Consent **Res. 228-26** (Sep 28 agenda) would amend/consolidate existing West Palm Beach Police Department contracts with **Axon Enterprise, Inc.** into one **eight-year** agreement not to exceed **$16,064,926**. Agenda background states Axon has been the department’s public-safety technology platform for over ten years, with agency-wide body-worn cameras, TASERs, in-car and interview-room systems, and related software.

## Why it matters for residents

This is a large, multi-year technology spend that affects how policing evidence and equipment are procured. Citizens can follow the consent item and later pass/fail or minutes for the recorded vote.
