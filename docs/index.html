West Palm Beach Civic Brief

A simple public site for West Palm Beach city data: demographics, City Commission meeting summaries, and an events calendar. Everything here comes from public sources and is cited.

This site is maintained independently. It does not speak for the City of West Palm Beach and does not write to any city systems.

  • Demographics

    ACS 2024 1-year snapshot for the city — population, housing, income, education, and related metrics with Census citations.

  • Commission

    Summaries of City Commission meetings linked to official wpb.org agenda PDFs (newest: Oct 13, 2026 DRAFT agenda — not final).

  • Wiki

    Cross-linked topics (budget/millage, mobility plan, housing, and more) tying meetings together.

  • Calendar

    Near-term Commission dates plus a downloadable ICS feed (“West Palm Beach City”).

Maintained by Aaron’s WPB team. Sources are cited on each page.

Last updated: 2026-10-04 (Oct 13 DRAFT agenda + WML calendar).
