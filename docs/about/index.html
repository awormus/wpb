About & methodology

West Palm Beach Civic Brief is a small, static public site for city data: demographics, City Commission meeting summaries, and a calendar. It is maintained by Aaron’s WPB team and hosted on GitHub Pages.

What we publish

  • Demographics derived from Census/ACS and similar public datasets
  • Commission meeting summaries grounded in official agendas and minutes
  • Calendar events and an ICS feed for personal use

Rules we follow

  • Public sources only. We use materials the City or other agencies already publish.
  • Read-only. We never write to, post on, or authenticate against city systems. External reads are logged in the repo under ops/external-access-log.md.
  • Cite everything. Pages and meeting notes name source documents and (when known) retrieval dates.
  • No original research claims. Summaries should be attributable and checkable against the cited sources.

Independence

This site is not an official City of West Palm Beach publication and does not speak for the City, elected officials, or city staff.

How content is updated

Editors and agents work in the awormus/wpb repository: structured data under content/, static pages under docs/. Changes are committed and pushed; GitHub Pages serves /docs from the main branch. See the repository README for the site map and isolation rules.

Source categories

Typical sources include: City of West Palm Beach official website and agenda portals; U.S. Census Bureau / data.census.gov; Florida state open data where relevant. Exact citations appear on each content page.
