3. OTHER BUSINESS

3.1. Swearing-in Ceremony for the re-elected and newly elected City Commissioners.

Originating Department

Mayor's Office

Background Information

The swearing-in ceremony for the re-elected and newly elected City Commissioners is being held pursuant to Sec. 2-1, Code of Ordinances, which reads: "Commencement of terms of office. The term of office of a newly elected mayor or newly elected city commissioner shall commence at 5:00 p.m. on the fourth Thursday immediately following the regular election."

The Oath of Office will be administered to the re-elected and newly elected City Commissioners: Cathleen Ward (District 1), Christy Fox (District 3), and Stephen Sylvester (District 5).

Special City Commission - Apr 02 2026

3.2. Election of the City Commission President.

Originating Department

Mayor's Office

Background Information

The election of the City Commission President shall take place pursuant to Sec. 2.05 of the City's Charter, which reads: "Induction and meetings.

After each annual city election, the newly elected members shall be inducted and assume office at a public meeting. At such meeting, the city commissioners shall elect one (1) of their number to the office of the president of the city commission, who shall serve at the pleasure of the city commission."

Fiscal Note

No fiscal impact.
