1. CALL TO ORDER

2. CIVILITY AND DECORUM

The City of West Palm Beach is committed to civility and decorum by its officials, employees and members of the public who attend this meeting. The City Code, Secs. 2-31(8), 2-31(18) and 2-31(22), provides in pertinent part:
- Officials shall be recognized by the Chair and shall not interrupt a speaker.
- Public comment shall be addressed to the City Commission as a whole and not to any individual on the dais or in the audience.
- Displays of anger, rudeness, ridicule, impatience, lack of respect and personal attacks are strictly prohibited.
- Unauthorized remarks from the audience, stamping of feet, whistles, yells and similar demonstrations shall not be permitted.
- Offenders may be removed from the meeting.
- Any person desiring to address the Commission shall file a written request with the city clerk prior to consideration of the matter by the Commission or prior to the public comment portion of a meeting. The person wishing to speak shall complete a comment card for each agenda item the person wishes to address, which shall include the person's full name, address, and the numbered agenda item. The person will not be recognized if the comment card is not completed.

Special City Commission - Sep 08 2025 - Tentative Budget & Millage Rate

Background Information

Section 200.065, Florida Statutes, requires among other things, that the City Commission of the City of West Palm Beach, Florida adopt tentative millage rates necessary to fund the tentative budget, after notice and public hearing, prior to adopting a final budget of final millage rates.

Resolution No. 210-25(F) adopts the FY 2025/26 tentative ad valorem rates of 8.1308 mills for operating purposes and 0.0580 mills for debt service on voter-approved general obligation debt for an aggregate millage of 8.1888. The tentative operating millage rate of 8.1308 mills is 5.39% above the calculated rolled back rate of 7.7152.

Resolution No. 211-25(F) adopts the tentative estimates of revenue and expenditures for FY 2025/2026. The total Citywide budget for all funds (excluding CRA and ECR) is $780,541,591. However, the operating millage mentioned previously relates only to the General Fund budget, which totals $283,563,050. The budget is balanced on the tentative millage rates discussed above.

Public Hearings: Section 200.065 Florida Statutes requires that a taxing authority hold two (2) Public Hearings to adopt the annual budget.

At this first Public Hearing, the City Commission will first hear public comment on the proposed millage rates and budget for FY 2025/2026.

After hearing the public comment, the Commission may discuss the FY 2025/2026 budget and make changes. The Commission will then adopt the tentative millage rates through Resolution No. 210-25(F) and then adopt the tentative budget for FY 2025/2026 through Resolution No. 21125(F).

A second Public Hearing to adopt the final millage rates and final budget for FY 2025/2026 is scheduled for September 24, 2025, at 5:01 PM and will be noticed in the newspaper.

Fiscal Note

These items set the tentative budget and millage rate.
