Background Information

The Seaboard Coast Line Railroad Passenger Station (commonly known as the Seaboard Train Station) is a historic, active passenger rail facility located in Downtown West Palm Beach along South Tamarind Avenue (Amtrak/Tri-Rail station building at 209 South Tamarind Avenue). The station campus spans approximately 203-209 South Tamarind Avenue.

Originally opened in 1925 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1973, the station functions as a key intermodal gateway providing access to Amtrak intercity rail and Tri-Rail commuter rail, with adjacent connections to Palm Tran’s Intermodal Transit Center (150 Clearwater Drive) and nearby intercity bus service (Greyhound and FlixBus at 205 South Tamarind Avenue).

The station was comprehensively restored and re-dedicated in 1991. The City is now advancing a preservation-focused resilience program to maintain continuous public use while protecting an irreplaceable transportation and cultural asset.

Recent condition assessments and project site visits by the City’s Engineering Services Department, Public Works, and Housing and Community Development (HCD) confirm that the station’s roof and related building envelope components have become major vulnerabilities.

A proposal from an architect identified the need for structural analysis, replacement of the existing roof, removal and replacement of existing gutters, and repairs of major interior damage caused by water intrusion.

Time is of the essence to prevent ongoing deterioration and property damage. The continuing water intrusion and aging building systems create operational, safety, and preservation risks for the facility and increase the likelihood of emergency repairs and service disruptions, especially during severe weather.

The U.S. Department of Transportation awards a Better Utilizing Investments To Leverage Development (BUILD) grant to fund projects that will have a significant local or regional impact and improve transportation infrastructure. BUILD grants are awarded on a competitive basis for planning or constructing surface transportation infrastructure projects that will improve safety, environmental sustainability, quality of

City Commission - Feb 17 2026

life, mobility and community connectivity, economic competitiveness, and opportunity including tourism, state of good repair, partnership and collaboration, and innovation. The minimum award is $5 million for urban capital projects. The application deadline for FY 2026 is February 24, 2026. The BUILD grant limits the Federal share to no more than 80 percent (80%) of total eligible project costs, thereby requiring applicants to provide a minimum 20 percent (20%) non-Federal match.

SCOPE OF WORK

City staff proposes to advance the work in a phased, preservationcompliant program that prioritizes life-safety, service continuity, and resilience. The consultant’s scope includes preliminary design and investigatory services, State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) coordination and submissions, grant assistance support (including detailed cost estimating), design development, contract documents through 100 percent (100%), permitting/bidding support, and construction administration. In addition to the consultant tasks above, the City’s construction program is planned. The construction scope includes full roof replacement and associated structural repairs; critical mechanical and electrical protections needed for resilient operations; hardening of exterior openings through window rehabilitation/replacement using historic-compatible approaches; and southern canopy removal and associated interface restoration to reduce storm-related exposure and water intrusion pathways. City Commission - Feb 17 2026

Ordinance/Resolution

ORDINANCE NO. 5165-26: AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, AMENDING THE CODE OF ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF WEST PALM BEACH, FLORIDA, AT CHAPTER 94 - ZONING AND LAND DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS, ARTICLE II – ORGANIZATION, ADMINISTRATION AND ENFORCEMENT, TO ADD SECTION 94-58 REQUEST FOR ACCOMMODATIONS; ESTABLISHING PROCEDURES FOR REQUESTING A REASONABLE ACCOMMODATION FOR FAIR HOUSING ACT (FHA) AND AMERICANS WITH DISIBLITY ACT (ADA) REASONS; DECLARING THIS AMENDMENT TO BE CONSISTENT WITH THE COMPREHENSIVE PLAN OF THE CITY; PROVIDING A CONFLICTS CLAUSE, A CODIFICATION CLAUSE, AND A SEVERABILITY CLAUSE; PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Staff Recommended Motion

Approve First Reading of Ordinance No. 5165-26 and schedule Second Reading for March 2, 2026.

This motion is based upon the factual testimony presented, the staff report, the recommendation of the Planning Board, and the findings that the proposed amendment complies with all the amendment standards found in Section 94-32 of the City's Zoning and Land Development Regulations.

Background Information

Currently, the City has established an informal process for Community Residence or persons who need to request a Reasonable Accommodation for Fair Housing Act (FHA) and/or Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) reasons. As of July 1, 2025, Section 397.487, Florida Statutes, now requires all government entities to provide a formalized process within their regulations. This text amendment outlines this process as well as provides the specific information required for submittal of a Reasonable Accommodation request.

The specific language for the text amendment is provided in the Ordinance, and the background is provided in the Planning Board staff report.

City Commission - Feb 17 2026

PLANNING BOARD

The Planning Board recommended approval (5-0) of this request to the City Commission after a Public Hearing on January 21, 2026, based on the testimony presented at the hearing, along with the application submitted and the Staff Report that the request complies with the standards for text amendments.
