Regular City Commission Meeting — November 9, 2026

When: November 9, 2026, 5:00 PM
Where: Commission Chambers, 401 Clematis Street
Source: Official final agenda PDF on wpb.org (linked below). This page summarizes what is scheduled, not vote outcomes.

Final agenda replaces the November 2 draft. Versus the draft, public hearing item 8.2 (Ordinance No. 5190-26, Breakwater Hospitality Group ground lease for a Currie Park restaurant) is not on the final agenda. Other numbered items and titles match the draft. Items 9.1 (Ordinance No. 5183-26) and 9.2 (Resolution No. 217-26), the CityPlace requests, remain the two quasi-judicial items the October 26, 2026 pass/fail agenda listed as continued to this meeting (Oct 26 pass/fail).

Opening

Call to order, moment of silence, pledge, civility/decorum, and any agenda additions/deletions/reorganization (see full text for exact wording).

Consent Calendar (6)

  • 6.1: Minutes of the September 8, 2026, Special City Commission Meeting.
  • 6.2: Minutes of the September 14, 2026, City Commission Meeting.
  • 6.3: Resolution No. 239-26 accepting a $15,000 FDOT litter-prevention grant for Keep West Palm Beach Beautiful; Resolution No. 238-26(F) appropriating the funds.
  • 6.4: Resolution No. 248-26(F) appropriating $146,000 from Federal Law Enforcement Forfeiture Receipts for investigative operating expenses.
  • 6.5: Resolution No. 249-26 approving a Utility Work by Highway Contractor Agreement with FDOT for incidental city utility work on the I-95 / Palm Beach Lakes Blvd interchange project (FDOT expense).
  • 6.6: Resolution No. 254-26(F) year-end budget clean-up amendments for FY 2025/2026.

Resolutions (7)

  • 7.1: Resolution No. 250-26 approving Tony Cragg sculptures "Points of View" for 10 & 15 City Place (public art requirement; 1% escrow $3,487,500).
  • 7.2: Resolution No. 251-26 adopting the 2026 Watershed Master Plan (CRS Class 4 prerequisite; FEMA CRS recertification audit November 13, 2026).

Public Hearing (8)

  • 8.1: First Reading of Ordinance No. 5188-26 extending the Publix Outside Closing Date on 202–206 North Sapodilla Avenue to May 26, 2027 (second reading set for November 23, 2026).

Public Hearing - Quasi-Judicial (9)

  • 9.1: First Reading of Ordinance No. 5183-26 amending CityPlace DRI Section 3.29 Small Business Enterprise language (continued from Oct 26).
  • 9.2: Resolution No. 217-26 Major Planned Development Amendment to CityPlace CPD (SBE language update, Helipad/Vertistop in Hibiscus Garage Subarea, misc. text; continued from Oct 26).
