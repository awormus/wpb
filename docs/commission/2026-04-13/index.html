City Commission — Regular Meeting

Monday, April 13, 2026 · Commission Chambers, 401 Clematis Street

Highlights below cite the official pass/fail sheet where outcomes are listed; full searchable text is in the repo.

Highlights

  • FPU gas franchise (Ord. 5148-25): 2nd reading not approved
  • Norton / Pioneer Park lease (Ord. 5167-26): 1st reading approved — Norton Museum art/cultural park
  • Broadway affordable housing (Res. 77-26): approved — five parcels; ~151 units ≤80% AMI
  • Tree canopy (Res. 87-26): 25% cover goal by 2035
  • Northwood Road Height Incentive (Res. 88-26(F)): $6.0M developer payment appropriated
  • Chase / Trinity streetscape (Res. 56-26): GMP revised to $8,988,810.56
