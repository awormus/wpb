City Commission — September 28, 2026

5:00 PM · Commission Chambers, 401 Clematis Street

This is an agenda-based summary of what was scheduled. It is not minutes and does not report votes or outcomes. Official PDF stays on wpb.org (we do not host a copy).

Official final agenda PDF (wpb.org) →

Proclamation

National Hispanic Heritage Month (Sep 15–Oct 15, 2026), with Heritage Honorees named by the Mayor and Commissioners.

Consent calendar (highlights)

  • Minutes of the July 20, 2026 regular meeting
  • PBSO forensic robotic mapping system donation (~$17,000) for police
  • IPTM/FDOT-related bike & pedestrian safety overtime grant (up to $36,716.22)
  • Three DDA patrol bicycles (~$10,320)
  • Axon Enterprise contract amendment — eight-year agreement not to exceed $16,064,926
  • Utility lien assessments ($14,050.32) for June 2026 unpaid charges
  • SHIP LHAP amendment adding Manufactured Housing Assistance Strategy
  • FY 2026–2027 Salary Plan
  • FOP Captains successor CBA (Oct 1, 2026–Sep 30, 2027); agenda background describes ~9% step-plan increase

Separate resolutions

  • Res. 188-26: Above-ground design features for lift stations at 145 Trinity Place and 2300 Village Boulevard (agenda cites $405,000 FDEP Resilient Florida support for part of the Trinity Place work)
  • Res. 214-26: Surplus a ~1,430 sq ft strip at 209 N. Sapodilla Ave. for conveyance toward a proposed 191-unit 100% affordable rental project (Related Ross)

Public hearings

  • Ord. 5182-26 (2nd reading): Adopt the 2050 Mobility Plan and citywide mobility-fee code amendments
  • Ord. 5184-26 (2nd reading): Clarify commercial solid-waste franchise rules (including a proposed 10-franchise limit)
  • Res. 205-26: Adopt updated City/CRA affordable-housing property inventory (§166.0451, F.S.)
  • Res. 208-26: Vacate a portion of Winters Street to support an 87-space off-site lot for Forest Hill Community High School
