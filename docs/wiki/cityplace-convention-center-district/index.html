CityPlace CPD — Convention Center District & Hotel II (Ord. 5179-26)

Categories: Land use & zoning

What this is

Ordinance No. 5179-26 amends the CityPlace Commercial Planned Development to adopt development regulations for the Convention Center District and Hotel II Subarea, with companion Major Planned Development resolutions (Res. 156-26 / 157-26) as noticed. Agenda background traces the CityPlace DRI to Res. 44-99 (Mar 8, 1999) amending Ord. 3098-97. A related Res. 186-26 Convention Center agreement amendment also appeared Aug 3.

Why it matters for residents

CityPlace / convention-center rules affect downtown hotel, event, and mixed-use buildout next to the convention center. Multiple August hearings (including a continuance) show the quasi-judicial track.
