Norton / Pioneer Park lease (Ord. 5167-26)

Categories: Parks & open space · Arts & culture

What this is

Ordinance No. 5167-26 authorizes a lease of City-owned Pioneer Park / Norton Park (green median at 1450 S. Olive Avenue, between S. Flagler Drive, Pioneer Plaza, Diana Place, and Acteon Place) to the Norton Museum of Art, Inc. for development and operation as a public art / cultural park that remains open to the public. Agenda/pass-fail extracts describe a 50-year term with a 20-year renewal option; base rent $100/year (with contingent additional rent after year 10); Norton authority over artworks and naming (subject to limitations); bollard/traffic-control rights on Diana and Actaeon Places for certain Friday–Saturday windows; community education programs; local-workforce and small-business participation targets; and construction completion deadlines. Sale/lease of park land requires super-majority approval.

Why it matters for residents

The median stays public-facing but under museum operation for art programming. Neighbors may care about hours, traffic controls, and long-term control of parkland.
