Publix Sapodilla sale (Ord. 5166-26)

Categories: Land use & zoning

What this is

Ordinance No. 5166-26 authorizes sale of City-owned property at 202–206 N. Sapodilla Avenue (~0.79 acres) to Publix Super Markets, Inc. for a full-service supermarket, purchase price $3,526,684, with a purchase and sale agreement. Code requires super-majority (4/5) approval at either first or second reading for this class of sale (§22-31(27) / related code cites on the agenda). Earlier, Res. 272-25 (Nov 10, 2025) found the three vacant parcels surplus.

Why it matters for residents

Disposes of city-owned downtown parcels for grocery retail. Surplus finding and sale ordinance are the two key Commission checkpoints.
