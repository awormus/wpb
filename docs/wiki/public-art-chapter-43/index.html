Public art program — Chapter 43 (Ord. 5172-26)

Categories: Arts & culture

What this is

Ordinance No. 5172-26 amends and restates the Art in Public Places (AiPP) program—relocating prior Chapter 78 Article V / monuments / murals provisions and creating Chapter 43 (Public Art). Agenda background cites Ord. 1913-85 (Dec 2, 1985) creating the AiPP Committee. Companion Res. 127-26 approved the Public Art Master Plan 2025–2030 (2026–2031) after staff/committee review of the prior five years.

Why it matters for residents

Public-art rules and the master plan steer how developer contributions and city projects fund artworks in public places. Chapter reorganization makes the code easier to find for applicants and residents.
