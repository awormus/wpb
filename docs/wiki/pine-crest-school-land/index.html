Pine Crest school land Term Sheet (Res. 161-26)

Categories: Schools & education · Land use & zoning

What this is

Res. 161-26 approves a Term Sheet with Pine Crest Preparatory School, Inc. for proposed sale of approximately 57 acres of City property south of Roebuck Road (adjacent to the Florida Turnpike) for a phased K–12 school. Agenda background states Pine Crest desires to develop, construct, and contract for operation of a kindergarten-through-grade-12 school on the site.

Why it matters for residents

Term Sheet approval is not itself the conveyance ordinance—expect later readings/agreements. It signals Commission direction on a large city land disposition for a private school campus.
