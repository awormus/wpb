5710 N. Haverhill annexation / Kolter

Categories: Land use & zoning · Housing

What this is

A package of Ords. 5174 / 5175 / 5176-26 (voluntary annexation and related land-use actions) plus Res. 136-26 (special site plan), requested by Urban Design Studios on behalf of owner Palm Lake Baptist Association Inc. and applicant The Kolter Group LLC. Agenda analysis describes an ~8.54-acre site at the NE corner of 45th Street and Haverhill Road in unincorporated Palm Beach County (addressed in packets as 5710 N. Haverhill area).

Why it matters for residents

Annexation brings the site into city jurisdiction for services, zoning, and taxes. Site-plan approval sets what can be built after annexation.
