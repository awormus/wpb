Sign regulations rewrite (Ord. 5180-26)

Categories: Land use & zoning

What this is

Ordinance No. 5180-26 is a city-initiated Chapter 94 zoning text amendment to update and replace existing sign regulations citywide. Agenda background states current signage regulations had not had a complete review and update since 1992, and that the City hired Jeff Katims of Complete Cities Planning Group to assist. Companion Ord. 5171-26 ROW amendments appeared on the same August agendas.

Why it matters for residents

Sign rules affect businesses, neighborhoods, and right-of-way clutter. A full rewrite is rare; citizens can compare the August 1st/2nd reading packets for substantive changes.
