Greene School CPD (Ord. 5169-26 / Res. 103-26)

Categories: Schools & education · Land use & zoning

What this is

Ordinance No. 5169-26 (with related Res. 103-26 site actions as noticed) rezones 5.38 acres from General Commercial (GC) to a Commercial Planned Development (CPD) for the Greene School. Agenda request text names Jon Schmidt and Christopher Claeyesen of Schmidt Nichols on behalf of Tobias Partners LP and Jeff Greene.

Why it matters for residents

CPD zoning sets site-specific development standards for the school campus versus standard GC rules.
