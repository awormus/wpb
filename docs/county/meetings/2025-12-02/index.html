Regular board meeting. 72 agenda items.

Scope: these pages list what was scheduled on official Palm Beach County Board of County Commissioners agendas. They do not show how anyone voted. Official per-member votes are recorded only in the Clerk & Comptroller’s final minutes, which are not online in bulk; they are available by request from the Clerk’s Board Services (561-355-2951, mypalmbeachclerk.com/records/minutes).
