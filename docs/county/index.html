Palm Beach County Commission

Agendas of the Palm Beach County Board of County Commissioners (BCC) since November 2018, plus who held each seat. West Palm Beach is the county seat, and many County decisions (Convention Center, roads, transit, housing money) land in the city.

What these pages are — and are not

  • Agenda items only. Each meeting page lists what was scheduled on the official agenda, with links to the County’s agenda PDF, HTML agenda and item backup PDFs. Agendas do not record outcomes or votes.
  • No per-member votes. How each commissioner voted is recorded only in the Clerk & Comptroller’s final BCC minutes. Those are not online in bulk: they are available only by request from the Clerk’s Board Services, 561-355-2951 (mypalmbeachclerk.com/records/minutes). The Clerk’s former “myMinutes” online search is no longer available.
  • Not included: BCC zoning and comprehensive-plan hearings (separate agendas from Planning, Zoning & Building), meeting video, and County advisory boards.
  • This is not an official County site. Everything here was read from public County and Supervisor of Elections pages, read-only.
