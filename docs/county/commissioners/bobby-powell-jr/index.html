Background

  • Elected to the County Commission in November 2024 after eight years in the Florida Senate and four years in the Florida House of Representatives; served as Senate Minority Leader Pro Tempore and chaired the Florida Legislative Black Caucus (2020–2022).
  • Professional land use planner (AICP); worked more than a decade as a project manager with Urban Design Studio.
  • Palm Beach County native and Palm Beach Gardens High School graduate; B.S. in Journalism, Florida A&M University; M.S. in Urban & Regional Planning, Florida State University.
