Background

  • Served 12 years on the Palm Beach County School Board before joining the County Commission.
  • Worked at the Palm Beach County Health Department; coordinated the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation Healthy Kids, Healthy Communities grant for the School District and served as a wellness coordinator in School Food Service.
  • B.S. in Health Science, University of Florida; Master of Public Health, Emory University. Lives in Lake Worth Beach.
