Gregg K. Weiss — District 2

Former commissioner, Palm Beach County Board of County Commissioners. Left office.

Votes: Per-member votes are not shown. How each commissioner voted is recorded only in the Clerk & Comptroller’s final BCC minutes, which are not online in bulk; they are available by request from the Clerk’s Board Services (561-355-2951, mypalmbeachclerk.com/records/minutes).

    Elected 2018 and re-elected 2022; the County Charter bars a commissioner from the ballot after eight consecutive years. Erica Whitfield took the District 2 seat on Nov 17, 2026. [Palm Beach County Charter, Sec. 2.2 (no reelection on the ballot after eight consecutive years)]

Background

  • Retired business executive from California, elected to the District 2 seat in November 2018 and re-elected in 2022.
  • Before the Commission, served as vice chair of the West Palm Beach Planning Board; was president of the West Palm Beach Police Foundation.
  • Graduate of the University of California, Irvine (economics and social ecology); career in the computer industry, overseeing sales, marketing and strategy for several Fortune 500 companies.
  • Moved to West Palm Beach after anchoring in the Lake Worth Lagoon in 2004 (per the page).
