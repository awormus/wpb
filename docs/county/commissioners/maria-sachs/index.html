Background

  • Served in the Florida House of Representatives (District 86, 2006–2010) and the Florida Senate (District 30, 2010–2012; District 34, 2012–2016) before the County Commission.
  • Former Assistant State Attorney in Miami-Dade under Janet Reno; law partner at Sachs, Augspurger & White, P.A. in Boca Raton.
  • Degrees from the University of Maryland (B.A.), Boston University (M.A.) and the University of Miami (J.D.); born in Battle Creek, Michigan.
