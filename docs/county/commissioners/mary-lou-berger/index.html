Background

  • New York native; moved to Palm Beach County in 1989.
  • Worked for the Palm Beach County Clerk of Court (Minutes Department supervisor) from 1989 to 1994, then as senior administrative assistant to County Commissioner Burt Aaronson in District 5 from 1994 to 2012.
  • Elected to the District 5 seat in November 2012 and re-elected in November 2016; the page lists past service as County Mayor and Vice Mayor.
  • Described District 5 as encompassing West Boynton, Boca and Delray Beach (per the page).
