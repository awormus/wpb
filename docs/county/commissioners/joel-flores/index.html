Background

  • Army combat veteran of Operation Enduring Freedom in Afghanistan (Combat Action Badge, Army Commendation Medal, Army Achievement Medal).
  • Mayor of the City of Greenacres from 2017 to 2024.
  • Bachelor’s in Business, Lyndon State College; MBA, Southern New Hampshire University.
  • Serves on the Criminal Justice Commission, Public Safety Coordinating Council, Transportation Planning Agency, Treasure Coast Regional Planning Council, Value Adjustment Board and Circuit 15 Juvenile Justice Advisory Board (per the page).
