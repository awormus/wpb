Background

  • A 20-year Boca Raton resident, elected in November 2022 to represent District 4.
  • The page lists County Commission representation on the Palm Beach MPO (Executive Committee), Tri-Rail, CareerSource Palm Beach County, the Cultural Council, the Solid Waste Authority (chair) and the Value Adjustment Board (vice chair).
  • South Carolina native; graduate of the University of South Carolina–Aiken.
  • The page’s contact block lists her title as Mayor (Nov 2026).
