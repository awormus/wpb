Background

  • Born at Good Samaritan Hospital; a second-generation local who lives in The Acreage.
  • Worked as a Certified Retinal Angiographer at the Bascom Palmer Eye Institute; in 2011 co-founded an auto repair business, Foreign Auto Specialists, with her husband.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Palm Beach State College.
