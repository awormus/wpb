Background

  • Elected to the Board of County Commissioners in 2014, served as County Mayor in 2017/2018, and was re-elected in 2018 without opposition.
  • Before her election, spent nearly twenty years as a legislative aide (Palm Beach County Legislative Affairs Office, members of Congress and the U.S. Senate).
  • Elected President of the Florida Association of Counties; served on the National Association of Counties Board of Directors (per the page).
  • Florida State University graduate (Political Science and Sociology).
