Background

  • Originally from Queens, New York; has lived in Palm Beach County since 1987.
  • Appointed by Governor Ron DeSantis to the County Commission on January 5, 2023, representing District 3 (Greenacres, Lake Worth Beach, Lake Clarke Shores, Palm Springs, Cloud Lake, Glen Ridge and parts of unincorporated Palm Beach County).
  • Personal injury attorney with the Shiner Law Group since 2017; J.D., University of Miami (2007); B.A., University of South Florida (2004); A.A., Palm Beach State College (2002).
  • Appointed by the Governor to the Florida Medical Examiners Commission (per the page).
