Background

  • Before the County Commission, served as State Representative for District 93 (Wellington, Greenacres and western Lake Worth).
  • Earlier elected to two terms on the Port of Palm Beach Commission, including as chair.
  • Business executive with Fortune 50 companies and small-business founder; B.A. in Economics, University of Virginia; MBA, Palm Beach Atlantic University. Palm Beach County resident since 2002.
  • Co-founded Palm Beach County Cares in 2017 (per the page).
