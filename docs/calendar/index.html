Holiday in Paradise — City events

Signature free events from the City’s Nov 9, 2026 news release (“City Kicks Off the Holiday Season with Holiday in Paradise”). Holiday in Paradise Presented by FPL runs Nov 27–Dec 31, 2026 (“The Art of Holidays”). Sandi’s music and light shows run nightly on the quarter hour 6–10 p.m. Dec 3–30 (through midnight on New Year’s Eve); Ferris wheel rides are $5. Clematis by Night has no event on Thursday, Dec 24 (holiday break).

  • Fri Nov 27, 2026 · 6:00–9:00 PM — Nights in Northwood — Holiday Edition (Jam-X The Band) — FREE

    Northwood Road, West Palm Beach, FL 33406. Open-air night market (50+ vendors) with live music from Jam-X The Band on the NewDay USA stage. Black Friday / day before Small Business Saturday. FREE. Also listed as a 4th Friday quarterly event on the City’s Nights in Northwood Village page (wpb.org/NIN). Sources: Nov 9 City news release and the Mayor’s Insider Newsletter (published Nov 13, 2026).

  • Thu Dec 3, 2026 · 6:00–10:00 PM — Clematis by Night Holiday Tree Lighting (Sandi debut) — FREE

    Great Lawn, 100 N. Clematis Street. Sandi’s season debut; Phoenix5 and Officers & Gentlemen with the Rhythm Chicks; live painting by Laura Serna 6–9 p.m.; Santa and Mrs. Claus, face painters, stilt walkers, gift boutique. Music/activities/boutique end at 9 p.m.; Sandi and Ferris continue to 10 p.m. Source: City news release.

  • Fri Dec 4, 2026 · 5:00–9:00 PM — Winter in Paradise — Rhythm on Rosemary — FREE

    Heart & Soul Park, 825 N. Rosemary Avenue. ReaXtion Band with DJ Scoobz; interactive games and family activities. Fire Rescue free hamburgers and hot dogs; Police free popcorn (while supplies last). Source: City news release.

  • Thu Dec 10, 2026 · 6:00–9:00 PM — Clematis by Night featuring Friday @Five — FREE

    Great Lawn. Friday @Five (Party Rock, Pop, Country) on the NewDay USA Stage. Source: City news release.

  • Thu Dec 17, 2026 · 6:00–9:00 PM — Aloha Islanders’ Holiday Spectacular at Clematis by Night — FREE

    Great Lawn. Polynesian holiday show with drums, hula, fire knife dancers, tropical Santa, and conch-blowing contest. Source: City news release.

  • Sun Dec 27, 2026 · 6:00–9:00 PM — Sandi’s Ice Cream Social — FREE to attend

    Waterfront / Lake Pavilion. Free to attend; ice cream for purchase. Silent disco in Centennial Square (free, first-come first-served), inflatables, Ferris wheel, bubble artist. Source: City news release.

  • Thu Dec 31, 2026 · 6:00 PM–midnight — Sandi’s New Year’s Eve (DJ Reflex & Wonderama) — FREE

    Great Lawn. Sandi shows 6 p.m.–midnight; party 8 p.m.–midnight with DJ Reflex and Wonderama. Food vendors on site; fireworks from Flagler Museum at midnight. Source: City news release.

Mandel Public Library

From the Mandel Public Library newsletter “It’s National Native American Heritage Month” (Nov 12, 2026). Dates, times, and rooms below are taken from the library’s public event pages (retrieved 2026-11-13). All events are at Mandel Public Library of West Palm Beach, 411 Clematis Street, unless a room is noted.

  • Nov 1–30, 2026 — National Native American Heritage Month programs

    All-day span for the month. Library programs tagged National Native American Heritage Month, including seed packets (plants traditionally used in Native American cooking; one per household, while supplies last) and other programs on the library calendar. Individual program times are on the tagged program list.

  • Fri Nov 13, 2026 · 2:00–3:00 PM — Capoeira with Mayling Pao

    Auditorium, 3rd floor. Afro-Brazilian martial art and dance; no experience necessary. Wear comfortable clothes and bring water. Generally meets every second and fourth Friday. Source: event page.

  • Sat Nov 14, 2026 · 2:00–3:00 PM — Craft Club (grades K-5)

    KidSpace Homework Center. Join Ms. Camila for a fun craft; dress to get messy. Space is limited; in-person sign-up begins in KidSpace at 1:30 p.m. Source: event page.

  • Sun Nov 15, 2026 · 2:00–3:30 PM — Piano Music from the Mystic World (Yoko Sata Kothari)

    Auditorium, 3rd floor. Classical pianist Yoko Sata Kothari presents piano music inspired by the supernatural. Source: event page.

  • Wed Nov 18, 2026 · 4:00–5:00 PM — Teen Writer’s Workshop: Gratitude and Thankfulness (grades 6-12)

    TeenSource, 2nd floor. Writing project focused on gratitude; beginners welcome. In-person sign-up begins at 3:30 p.m. Source: event page.

  • Wed Nov 18, 2026 · 6:30–7:30 PM — Get More Done With AI Agents

    Clematis Room, 3rd floor. Live demo of practical AI-agent tools (no coding). Organizer: Career and Technology Programs. Source: event page.

  • Sat Nov 21, 2026 · 3:00–4:00 PM — Jane Austen Society of North America talk with Lauren Gilbert

    Clematis Room, 3rd floor. Lauren Gilbert on what respectable folk might have done for amusement in London. Source: event page.

  • Mon Nov 23, 2026 · 1:00–3:00 PM — Winter Reading Kickoff Party

    KidSpace, 3rd floor. For kids of all ages: games, crafts, sign-up for Winter Reading, and community partners. Source: event page.

  • Mon Nov 23, 2026 · 4:30–5:30 PM — Palm Beach Opera Story Time: Hansel and Gretel (family sing-along)

    Kids Programming Space. For families with kids of all ages; operatic retelling of the fairy tale. Space is limited; in-person sign-up begins in KidSpace at 4 p.m. Source: event page.

  • Dec 1, 2026–Jan 31, 2027 — Winter Reading Program: Starry Nights and Reading Lights

    All-day span. Teens can earn free books by logging reading in the Beanstack app or website; prize drawings also listed. The event page lists the audience as All Ages. Source: event page.

City services & notices

From the Mayor’s Insider Newsletter (published Nov 13, 2026) and related City pages, also cited from the City email “News & Events in the City of West Palm Beach” (Nov 13, 2026).

  • Tue Nov 17, 2026 — Bulk and yard waste collection resumes (Briar Bay / Hamilton Bay)

    All-day service notice. The City’s Nov 12 flood-response news release says bulk and yard waste collection will resume Tuesday, November 17, and that residents will not be charged for excess bulk or yard waste accumulated during the flooding-related interruption. The Mayor’s newsletter specifies that collection resumes that day in Briar Bay, Hamilton Bay, and neighboring areas.

  • Fri Nov 20, 2026 · 5:00 PM deadline — West Palm Beach Business Survey (TIP Strategies)

    The City invites its business establishments to complete a confidential survey conducted on the City’s behalf by TIP Strategies. Responses must be completed by 5 p.m. on November 20, 2026. Public survey: West Palm Beach Business Survey. Source: Mayor’s Insider Newsletter.

  • Apr 13–14, 2027 — Tomorrow.City USA (Smart City Expo World Congress)

    All-day span. West Palm Beach hosts the expo for the second consecutive year. Dates confirmed on tomorrowcity.us and in the Mayor’s newsletter. The official visit page describes a downtown conference and refers to “the venue” and “the convention center” but does not give a formal venue name or street address (retrieved 2026-11-14).

  • Jan 9–Feb 20, 2027 — Youth Winter / Spring Soccer League (Winter Junior Soccer 2027)

    All-day span for the confirmed winter session. On parks.wpb.org the program is Youth Winter / Spring Soccer League (211103). Winter dates listed for ages 4–6: Jan 9–Feb 20, 2027, Saturdays 9:00–10:00 a.m., Howard Park Community Center, 1302 Parker Avenue ($60 resident / $75 non-resident). No registration deadline was listed on the parks pages retrieved 2026-11-14; registration is open. The same listing has spring sections for ages 7–14 from March 9–May 13, 2027. The newsletter also says you can register in person at a City community center or by calling (561) 804-4900. Source: Mayor’s Insider Newsletter.
